What data we process and why

You do not need to provide any personal data to browse the Website. We only process personal data when you give it to us:

Sending it creates an anonymous identifier (Firebase Authentication), with no personal data, which lets us limit submissions and prevent spam; it is deleted automatically after 30 days.

We do not create profiles, make automated decisions or use your data to send you advertising without your consent.

Legal basis

The legal basis for processing your data is the consent you give when you send us your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, when you ask us for information about a trip, taking pre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
