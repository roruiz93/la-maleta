What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites save in your browser. There are other similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), which also allow information to be stored on your device. In this policy we call all of them “cookies”.

What does this Website use?

This Website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It only stores in your browser's local storage the technical and preference information needed for the website to work as you expect. This data does not identify you, is not shared with third parties and does not leave your device:

NamePurposeTypeDuration
web_langRemember the language you chosePreferences (first-party)Until you delete it
web_lang_default, web_lang_v2Show the website in the default language if you have not chosen oneTechnical (first-party)Until you delete it
web_colors, web_logo, web_contactoInstantly show the agency's colours, logo and contact detailsTechnical (first-party)Until you delete it
contacto_enviosLimit the number of consecutive contact form submissions (spam protection)Technical / security (first-party)10 minutes
firebase:authUser (IndexedDB)Anonymous identifier created only when you send the contact form, to limit submissions (spam protection). It contains no personal dataTechnical / security (first-party)Until you delete it (the identifier is removed from our systems after 30 days)

In accordance with Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI) and the guidelines of the Spanish Data Protection Agency, these technologies are exempt from consent because they are strictly necessary to provide the service you request or to remember your preferences. That is why this Website does not show a cookie banner.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase: serves the pages and content of the Website. It does not set tracking cookies.
  • Cloudinary and Unsplash: serve some images. They do not set tracking cookies.
  • WhatsApp: only if you press the WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens and applies its own cookie policy.

How to delete or block this data

You can delete the stored information at any time by clearing this site's browsing data in your browser. The website will keep working; it will simply be shown in the default language again. Instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari

If in the future we add cookies that require your consent (for example, analytics cookies), we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
