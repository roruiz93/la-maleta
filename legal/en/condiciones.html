Trips operated by Viajes La Maleta S.L. (GC993), based in Arenys de Munt (Tax ID B62582556), are subject to the package travel regulations established by Spanish Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 of 16 November, as amended by Royal Decree-law 23/2018 of 21 December.

Changes

The organising agency may cancel a departure when the number of people registered is lower than the number stated in the programme. The order of the programme may also be changed when this benefits our customers or for justified reasons related to the booking.

Bookings

To book the trips listed in the “Groups and Seniors” section of our website, you must request a place and pay a deposit, stating the name of the person paying it, within 10 days. Otherwise, the place on the trip will not be guaranteed.

Cancellation of coach trips

If the traveller withdraws from the requested services, they will be entitled to a refund of the amount paid, but must compensate the agency for the stated management fees plus the corresponding penalty depending on the notice given:

  • 5% of the total amount, 60 days before departure.
  • 15% of the total amount, 30 days before departure.
  • 25% of the total amount, 15 days before departure.
  • 50% of the total amount, 48 hours before departure.
  • 100% of the total amount in case of no-show on the day of departure.

Cancellation of trips by plane, high-speed train (AVE) or cruise

If the traveller withdraws from the requested services, they will be entitled to a refund of the amount paid, but must compensate the agency for the stated management fees plus the corresponding penalty depending on the notice given:

  • 25% of the total amount, between 60 and 46 days before departure.
  • 50% of the total amount, between 45 and 30 days before departure.
  • 75% of the total amount, between 29 and 15 days before departure.
  • 100% of the total amount, 14 days before departure.

Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Insurance exclusions

The insurance does not cover the agency's management fees or the cost of the insurance itself.

Management fees

For all trips in the “Groups and Seniors” section of our website (unless otherwise stated in writing at the time of booking), an additional charge will apply depending on the total amount of the booking:

  • €35.00 per person for bookings of €500.00 or less.
  • €45.00 per person for bookings over €500.00 and under €1,000.00.
  • €90.00 per person for bookings over €1,000.00.

Assistance insurance

All our trips of two days or more include basic assistance insurance from departure until return.

Day trips

Day trips do not include travel insurance. In this case, the customer is covered by the current conditions of the Social Security scheme that applies to them.
