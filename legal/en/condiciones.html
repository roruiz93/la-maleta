Trips operated by Viajes La Maleta, S.L. – GC993, based in Arenys de Munt (Tax ID B62582556), are subject to the package travel regulations established by Spanish Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 of 16 November, as amended by Royal Decree-law 23/2018 of 21 December.

Changes

The organising agency may cancel a departure when the number of people registered is lower than the minimum stated in the programme. The order of the programme may also be changed when this benefits our customers or for justified reasons related to the booking.

Bookings

To book the trips listed in this catalogue, you must have requested a place and paid the corresponding deposit, stating the name of the registered traveller. Your place on the trip will not be guaranteed until the corresponding payment has been received.

Cancellation of coach trips

If the registered traveller withdraws from the requested services, they will be entitled to a refund of the corresponding amount, but must pay the agency the stated management fees. In addition, the corresponding penalty will apply depending on when the booking is cancelled:

  • 5% of the total amount, from the time of booking.
  • 15% of the total amount, between 60 and 31 days before departure.
  • 25% of the total amount, between 30 and 8 days before departure.
  • 50% of the total amount, between 7 and 2 days before departure.
  • 100% of the total amount, from 1 day before departure and in case of no-show.

Cancellation of trips by plane, high-speed train (AVE) or cruise

If the registered traveller withdraws from the requested services, they will be entitled to a refund of the corresponding amount, but must pay the agency the stated management fees. In addition, the corresponding penalty will apply depending on when the booking is cancelled:

  • 5% of the total amount, from the time of booking.
  • 25% of the total amount, between 60 and 46 days before departure.
  • 50% of the total amount, between 45 and 30 days before departure.
  • 75% of the total amount, between 29 and 15 days before departure.
  • 100% of the total amount, from 14 days before departure.

Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Insurance

The agency's management fees and the cost of the insurance itself are not covered by the insurance.

Management fees

For all trips in this catalogue, unless otherwise stated in writing at the time of booking, an additional charge will apply depending on the total amount of the booking:

  • €35 per person for bookings of €500 or less.
  • €45 per person for bookings over €500 and under €1,000.
  • €90 per person for bookings over €1,000.

Assistance insurance

All our trips of two days or more include basic assistance insurance, from departure until the end of the trip.

Day trips

Day trips do not include travel insurance. In this case, customers must rely on the healthcare cover available to them.

Food intolerances

Any food intolerance must be reported at the time of booking. Once the trip has started, no changes can be made to the menus.

Personal belongings

Viajes La Maleta is not responsible for any items forgotten or lost by customers during the trips.

Prices

Trip prices were calculated on the publication date of this programme (04.09.2026), based on currency exchange rates, transport prices resulting from the cost of fuel or other energy sources, and the taxes and fees applicable to the travel services included in the programme. Prices may be changed up to 20 days before departure.
